Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Platform: FieldOps

1. Information We Collect

FieldOps processes operational data essential for technician dispatching, attendance tracking, and work order completion:

  • Staff Information: Technician names, organizational email addresses, phone contacts, skill certifications, and operational roles.
  • Daily Dispatch Records: Team rosters (3 to 5 crew assignments), assigned dispatch vehicles, sector zones, and check-in timestamps.
  • Performance Logs: Aggregated numbers of installations completed and troubleshoot cases resolved per shift.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Information gathered through the Platform is strictly utilized to:

  • Enable real-time team assignment and attendance rollups for field crews.
  • Calculate payroll performance metrics and technician attribution.
  • Analyze root-cause equipment issues and service delivery trends.
  • Maintain operational accountability across residential and commercial deployments.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect personnel data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All database queries utilize parameterized SQL and role-based access control.

4. Data Retention and Export

Operational records are retained in accordance with corporate data retention schedules and statutory payroll requirements. Authorized administrators may export comprehensive audit records or purge historical staging data as required.

5. Contact Us

For questions or requests concerning your personal information or data privacy rights, contact your organization's Field Operations Administrator.
