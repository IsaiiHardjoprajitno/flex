Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Platform: fieldops.app

1. Operational Data Collection

FieldOps processes staff rosters, role assignments, vehicle tags, and work order metrics (installations and troubleshoot cases) exclusively for authorized operational management and dispatch.

2. Use of Information

Information is utilized to synchronize daily crew attendance, tabulate team productivity rollups, and ensure verifiable service delivery for field operations.

3. Data Protection Safeguards

We employ role-based access control, parameterized database transactions, and secure communication channels to ensure personnel and operational records remain confidential.

4. Questions & Inquiries

For questions or data access requests, contact your designated Field Operations Administrator at privacy@fieldops.app.
